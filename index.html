Welcome to Tic-Tac-Toe!

It's the classic game you know and love, but with a sleek new look. The goal is simple: be the first to get three of your marks in a row—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Game Rules

  1. The game is played on a 3x3 grid.
  2. You are X, and your opponent (the computer or another player) is O.
  3. Players take turns placing their marks in empty squares.
  4. The first player to get 3 of their marks in a row wins.
  5. If all 9 squares are filled and no one has won, the game is a draw.
